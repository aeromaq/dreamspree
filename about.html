About us

Dreamspree Travel & Tours: since 2014.

Dreamspree Travel & Tours delivers a complete range of travel products and services, with a diverse selection of high-quality options for travelers across the globe. Our journey began in 2014 with a small group, and today we look after larger groups traveling to destinations at home and abroad.

We keep improving our products by staying up to date with the latest travel trends. Our commitment is to offer affordable, high-quality, faster, hassle-free and efficient service.

We specialise in designing budget-friendly yet exciting and enjoyable packages, from land tours to domestic and international trips, that meet the needs of even the most discerning traveler.

Our most valuable asset is our team: experienced, trained travel consultants who bring a high degree of expertise and personal passion to your travels.

Our primary goal is to deliver safe, efficient and reliable travel experiences that you will remember for the rest of your life. We aim to consistently deliver the best in customer satisfaction, within a workplace culture that fosters excellent customer service.

Travel assurance

How Dreamspree verifies travel partners

Every partner should be checked before you ever meet them. We are proposing a written standard for doing that, and showing you the result on every listing.

Proposed standard

Business permits checked

Local business registration and permits confirmed as current before a partner is onboarded.

Relevant accreditation reviewed

Department of Tourism accreditation and other relevant certifications reviewed where they apply to the service.

Vehicle documentation

For transport partners, vehicle registration and insurance documents checked and kept on file.

Driver and guide screening

Background checks and licence verification for drivers and guides who will be with our travelers.

Test booking before listing

A Dreamspree team member books and experiences the service anonymously before it appears in a package.

Periodic re-audits

Listings would show the month of the most recent re-audit, and partners are reviewed on a regular cycle.

Dreamspree Verified is a proposed programme. This page describes the intended standard. No accreditation numbers, partner inspection records or completed verification claims are shown in this prototype; badges would appear on listings only once the programme is operating and records exist.

The Dreamspree Promise · concept

Vetted, inspected, and backed by people who answer.

The idea behind the Promise is simple: if a Dreamspree-arranged service fails to show, your consultant works to rebook it or make it right, rather than leaving you to a dispute form. The exact terms, timelines and any compensation are being defined and are not yet offered.

Concept only. Not a legally binding guarantee, refund deadline or compensation commitment. Final policy details will be published once approved.
